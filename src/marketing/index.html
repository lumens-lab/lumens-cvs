Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Designed with obsessive attention to detail. Every pixel serves a purpose.

AI-Powered Insights

AI analyzes spending patterns and delivers personalized recommendations to optimize your finances.

Bank-Grade Security

256-bit encryption, biometric authentication, and SOC 2 compliance protect every transaction.

Smart Goals

Set savings goals with intelligent projections and automated rounding to reach them faster.

Instant Transfers

Send money anywhere in seconds. No hidden fees, no delays, no intermediaries.

Stellar Settlement

5,000 TPS at $0.00001/tx. XLM, USDC, XRP and multi-asset swaps via the Stellar DEX.

Cross-Border Payments

Send and receive across 50+ currencies with real-time FX rates and zero markup.

Virtual Cards

Generate unlimited virtual cards for online purchases, subscriptions, and business expenses.

Built-in Messenger

Chat, share payment requests, and split bills — all without leaving the app.

DeFi Wallet

Self-custody crypto wallet supporting BTC, ETH, XLM, SOL, and 20+ more assets.
